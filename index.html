              Still not sure, or many plants are sick?

              Bring several fresh sick leaves (and the stem if it is affected) in a paper or plastic bag, together with this screen, to your Municipal Agriculture Office. They can send samples to a plant clinic such as the Bureau of Plant Industry or UPLB.

              This is a research prototype and can be wrong. Before you spray or remove plants, confirm the diagnosis with an agricultural technician, and use only pesticides registered with the Fertilizer and Pesticide Authority (FPA), following the label.

                How to take a good photo

                • One leaf that fills most of the square, showing the side with the spots.
                • Hold the phone flat above the leaf and keep still. Tilted or blurry photos are often wrong.
                • Even light: shade or near a window, no harsh shadows or dark corners.
                • Plain background if you can, such as paper, a table or your palm.
                • If the result is not sure, add 2 or 3 leaves from the same plant. More leaves give a more reliable answer.
