              Tips for an accurate result

              • One leaf, filling most of the square.
              • Plain background such as paper, a table or your palm.
              • Good, even light: shade or indoors near a window, no harsh shadows.
              • Sharp focus: tap the leaf on your screen before taking the photo.
              • Show the side with the spots or discoloration.
